Northstar Field Notes

Northstar Field Notes is a lightweight research field notebook for small product teams.

What the notebook covers

The notebook publishes concise field guides about planning interviews, organizing observations, and sharing evidence with collaborators.

Who it is for

It is designed for product researchers and early-stage teams that want a lightweight, evidence-first workflow.

How the evidence-first workflow works

Teams capture one observation at a time, attach its source, separate facts from interpretations, and review the resulting notes together before making a product decision.

Frequently asked question

Does Northstar Field Notes replace a research repository?

No. It is a lightweight field notebook for gathering and discussing evidence before selected findings move into a team's system of record.
